Gate 1 (observation fixes), Rengar deck: report, 2026-11-19
===========================================================
Input format is now SCHEMA v2. Every earlier checkpoint is invalid. This is stated in
selfplay/rbenv/README.txt and smoke/data/SCHEMA.txt.
- rbenv dims went from G72 T64 F17 A96 K46 to G72 T96 F24 A96 K49; V stays 80.
- New token kind 4 (pile).
- rbenv_schema_version() == 2.
The encoder change is in one place, smoke/data/src/decision_export.cpp (Encoder), which
rbenv includes. Nothing under src/ was edited, nothing was committed and nothing was downloaded.

What is left
------------
- The Sabotage-recycled card's known place at the bottom of the opponent's deck is not
  exported (A9). Neither is the opponent's unseen pool (DeckModel). Only the own deck
  composition is exported.
- Effective cost leaves out Accelerate and the optional extra costs (Rampage [O], Pyke [R]).
  These are separate prompts, and "affordable" uses canAfford, which allows for additional costs.
- Star Spring's stamp is read by its key (__starspring_turn_p<i>). Rampage paid reads
  __rampage_paid and the generic kAdditionalCostPaid flag. Other cards' private counters
  (other "first time" stamps) would need their keys added.
- The Location mapping assumes the Thrill pattern: an index over battlefieldTakesUnitsFrom
  for the chooser. No other value-answered Location prompt is in scope.
- Status flags not exported: cant_be_moved_by_enemy, moveToBaseBlocked, facedown_max_occupancy.
  The gate-0 audit lists these as minor.
- Data to regenerate for v2: the v1 probe npz files (learner/probes); the decision dataset
  smoke/data/out (still v1, and `decision_export verify` on it will now mismatch); and the
  exporter's swap mode, which writes -1 costs because it has no engine.
- Gates 2-5 are not started.
