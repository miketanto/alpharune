Layout (3,019 dims; full listing in schema.md)
----------------------------------------------
Card header:
- one-hots: type, supertype, domains, keywords;
- numeric: energy, power, Might, keyword values (Assault, Hunt, Level, Shield, Deflect N).

Ability slots: 3, in graph order. Each has:
- present, kind, timing;
- trigger event, subject and once;
- trigger conditions and ability conditions (condition x polarity);
- play mode, where-zone and where-filters;
- may (optional cost), cost flags;
- numeric cost energy and cost power;
- play_requires.

Effect slots: 4 per ability, in graph order. Each has:
- verb;
- ref (none / choice0..2 / subject);
- target side, type, zone, count;
- binds;
- relative_to (ref, side, type);
- filters (x polarity);
- chooser, duration, dest, may;
- effect conditions (x polarity);
- amount kind (n / might_of:choiceK / self), amount per;
- source, keyword;
- numeric amount (clipped to ±10) and token Might.

Binding:
- A ChooseTarget whose only job is to bind an object is folded out of the grid.
- Every reference to a bound object (target ref, relative_to, might_of:X, source) is replaced by:
  - the bound selector's side and type;
  - the choice index, which is the order of binding inside the ability.
- So with Rampage's binds swapped, the +2 slot reads "enemy unit, choice1".
- Rampage has 5 effects, 2 of them binds, so after folding the maximum is 3 effects per ability. Nothing overflows the grid.

Other encoding rules:
- Slot position carries effect order and ability order.
- Every categorical field in a present slot emits a value ("none" when absent), so a change is a flip of
  two coordinates.

Slot signatures. Each slot gets a 64-dim unit code seeded by the MD5 of the slot's whole content:
- one for the card header;
- one for each ability header;
- one for each effect slot.
Any edit inside a slot moves its code by about √2. Without them, the most any weighting reached was a
minimum rel of 0.19 (see below).

Vocabularies:
- Derived from 91 B2 graphs: cards, every engine class, both token units, all synthetic variants.
- Plus the schema-documented values (chooser controller / opponent / you, Shield / Deflect, and so on).
- Out-of-vocabulary values in use: 2, both in B's text graphs (source choice1, unit_is_token/neg). They go to <other>.

Scaling: the choice and the trade-off
-------------------------------------
Exact decomposition of the squared distance:
  d^2 = d_hash^2 + ALPHA^2 * sum_g w_g^2 d_g^2

How the weights were chosen:
- Weights were searched to maximise the minimum rel over all 1,128 pairs and the 53 edits (raw space).
- The Gaussian projection keeps the ratios; gate2 then checks init.

What the search showed:
1. One-hot / numeric fields alone, with any weights: minimum rel 0.19, and 23 items below 0.25.
   It is zero-sum, as v1 predicted. Raising side, duration and chooser crushes Might, amount and the runes.
2. Adding slot signatures makes one-word edits as large as replacing the whole effect.
   - With signatures unconstrained, the optimiser drove the hash to 0% and reached a minimum of 0.44.
   - So the hash share was fixed instead, then the signature share was capped.

Trade-off curve (raw min rel; then init min rel and the model-view T3 R² for non-provisional / provisional labels):

| signature share of typical d^2 | raw min rel | init min rel | T3 R² (non-prov / prov) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0.42 (uncapped, hash 50%) | 0.33 | 0.303 | 0.485 / 0.332 |
| 0.30 (hash 50%) | 0.30 | 0.289 | 0.595 / 0.406 |
| 0.25 (hash 60%) **CHOSEN** | 0.29 | 0.274 | 0.600 / 0.444 |
| 0.20 (hash 50%) | 0.26 | 0.238 FAIL | 0.661 / 0.509 |

The pre-block value of T3 R² was 0.639 / 0.608.

CHOSEN:
- the hash keeps 60% of the typical squared distance;
- the signatures take at most 25%;
- one-hot and numeric fields take the rest;
- ALPHA = 1.09; weights in struct_block.WEIGHTS.
- Among those passing at init with some margin, it is the setting that keeps the most cross-card
  generalisation (T3).
- The lowest init items sit at 0.27–0.29:
  - Rampage "+2 unconditional": 0.274;
  - Might +1: 0.280;
  - Darius duration: 0.280;
  - chooser: 0.289;
  - trigger subject: 0.292.

Model side:
- The card LayerNorm and gain lift the card from 9.1 to 37.5 of the token's norm. The rest of the token is 39.4.
- Typical card distance as a share of the token norm: 0.17 → 0.69.
- After the first LayerNorm: 0.16 → 0.49.
- The card LN itself lowers a few C2 rels by up to about 15% against init_lin (e.g. Rampage ungate: 0.315 → 0.274).

- The T3 R² drop on the raw vectors is mostly an artefact of eval.py's per-dimension standardisation.
  - It ignores the block weights and weighs every one of the 960 signature dims like a hash dim.
  - Without the signature dims the same vectors give T3 R² 0.322 / 0.286, the pre-block level.

- This is the price of the slot signatures. They are identity-like per slot, so the same edit in two cards
  points in different directions.

What's left
-----------

   - The bar is a 5-seed mean; the per-seed spread was not measured.
   - Each new card, or vocabulary entry, re-derived changes the typical distance.

   - an auxiliary contrastive loss;
   - lowering the signature cap once training shows the trained W keeps the margins.
