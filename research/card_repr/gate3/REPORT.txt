Gate 3 (wiring tests, TESTPLAN D1-D3), Rengar mirror: report, 2026-11-19
=========================================================================
Verdict: the asking-card bug class was present at 5 more prompt kinds, and it is now fixed and
verified for them. Two kinds still FAIL because of observation gaps that need exporter work (TriggerOrder,
MoveJoin). Accelerate / AdditionalCost now carry their cost consequences (schema v3, coordinator request),
and the network learns from them.

The bug, found again. The --ask-head term is W_ask(h_ask) * (h_ref + W_ask_src(h_src)). For an option that
references no token, this is exactly 0. That covers the yes/no of May / Accelerate / AdditionalCost, Location's
destinations and TriggerOrder's values. For those options the asking card reached the scores only through the
global token, which is added equally to every option. Only the pol MLP's nonlinearity keeps the effect from
cancelling. Measured effect on the option distribution when the asking card's key is swapped (TV):
- these 4 kinds: 1e-4 untrained, 4e-4 to 2.5e-3 after 5 min of training;
- Target: 0.015 untrained, 0.23 trained, i.e. 100-500x more;
- the asking unit's own live fields at those prompts (Might, exhausted, cost, ...): about 2e-4.

A synthetic supervised test is the decisive check. Labels depended only on the asking card, and fitting
reached only 0.54-0.63 after 300 steps for May, Accelerate and AdditionalCost. That means the path was
effectively missing. Resources (pool Energy) feeding the yes/no failed the same way, through the global token.

A. learner/model.py, Net(..., ask_opt=False). Needs --ask-head; selfplay.py --ask-opt.
   - h_opt is the option's own features: intent kind, value v and the scalar flags (W_v), destination,
     named card, and the v3 cost fields.
   - The ask term becomes W_ask(h_ask) * (h_ref + W_ask_src(h_src) + W_ask_opt(h_opt)). Options with no token
     refs now get an asking-card-dependent, option-specific term.
   - A global-token x option term is added: W_ctx(hg) * W_ctx_opt(h_opt). Before it, resources, pk and turn
     reached a yes/no only through the pol MLP's nonlinearity.
B. Schema v3 (coordinator request). Accelerate and AdditionalCost answers carry their cost consequences:
   - Encoder::optCost in smoke/data/src/decision_export.cpp, shared by rbenv; JSON key "oc", rbenv act[49..55].
   - K goes from 49 to 56, and rbenv_schema_version() is 3. Nothing under src/ was edited.
   - Fields, per option: extra Energy; extra Power; runes the play recycles; their Domain; own runes ready after
     paying; own runes in total after paying; unit enters ready (Accelerate).
   - Cost source: the base cost is GameEngine::netCardCost (the same as card fields 19-20). Accelerate adds
     [1] + one Power of the unit's Domains (CR 805.1.b.1). AdditionalCost reads Card::optionalAdditionalCost
     (Rampage's [O]: Power 1 Body).
   - model.py opt_cost (default on when K >= 56; selfplay.py --no-opt-cost turns it off): W_oc encodes the
     fields into h_opt, so they reach the score directly and through both bilinear terms.
   - Checked against the engine in random play (verify_optcost.py; the deciding seat's runes on the board at
     its next decision in the same turn):
     - runes in total after paying and runes recycled: 100% match (Accelerate yes 258, no 303; AdditionalCost

1. TriggerOrder: each option names its chain item (schema v4)
-----------------------------------------------------------
- Each option now carries act[33] / JSON "ci", the chain-item token of the item it puts on the chain next.
  It also carries act[1] / "src", that item's source card.
- No engine change was needed. Encoder::prepare (smoke/data/src/decision_export.cpp) rebuilds
  GameEngine::orderPendingTriggers' "distinct" list from the state:
  - before the prompt, the batch is already rewritten into canonical order on the chain (canonicalBatchOrder);
  - the options keep that order, and option k = distinct[k];
  - so the encoder takes the decider's pending items of the oldest pending batch, in chain order, and matches
    them to the option labels in order ("Order - " + the label, Encoder::trigLabel, a mirror of the private
    triggerOrderLabel), skipping items that are the same ability as one already matched.
- Exact except one case: two different items share a label and the earlier one was already ordered in an
  earlier prompt. rbenv counts such options (counters() trig_amb): 16 of 7,359 options in 300 random games (0.2%).
  Every option was mapped (trig_mapped == trig_opts; test_fields: 0 unmapped, 0 duplicate items, and the
  ci token's source == act[1] on all 7,351 options).

2. MoveJoin: destination, consequences, moving units, decline (schema v4)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
Every MoveJoin option now carries:
- act[30] / "dst": the move's destination;
- act[46..48] / "dc", "atk", "con": the consequences for the mover, computed as for a StandardMove;
- act[56..59]: the units already moving, leading unit first (first 4), with act[60] = their count
  (-1 for other prompts). The JSON "mg" has the whole list.
- "Move no more units" is marked as the decline (act[39] / "dec" = 1).
The engine keeps the destination and the moving group in locals of executeStandardMove. The caller therefore
follows the decider's own choices (MoveTrack / noteChoice: the StandardMove chosen, then each unit joined):
- in decision_export's Seat, replay included;
- at every applyChoice in rbenv (applyTracked, per seat; opponent and auto choices included).
test_fields over 300 games / 9,675 MoveJoin options:
- 0 without a destination or a group;
- exactly one dec option per prompt;
- consequences identical across a prompt's options;
- the group is the mover's own units and never includes the offered unit.
Truncation: groups larger than 4 are cut to their first 4 units. This affects 1.3% of MoveJoin options in random
play and is all of rbenv's trunc_list (78 in 200 games). act[60] still holds the full count.

Layout: rbenv K 56 -> 61, rbenv_schema_version() == 4. Docs: smoke/data/SCHEMA.txt "SCHEMA VERSION 4",
selfplay/rbenv/README.txt. Backups: decision_export.v3.cpp.bak, rbenv.v3.cpp.bak, model.v3.py.bak (this dir).

5. Runes ready after paying (act 53): now replays the engine's rune order
--------------------------------------------------------------------------
Encoder::optCost now pays rune by rune in the engine's own order, which autopay (option 0) takes:
1. The [O] cost first: resource::payIn. Energy from ready runes that cannot pay its Power, lowest id first;
   Power from paying runes, exhausted first, lowest id first.
2. Then the card's cost through the cost cursor. Energy from ready runes in base in object-table order
   (readyRunesInBase); Power from the first rune of the card's Domains in that order, ready or exhausted
   (matchingRunesInBase). Accelerate's [1][C] is in this payment.
Before, the [O] Domain also replaced the card's Domains for the base Power. That bug is fixed too.
