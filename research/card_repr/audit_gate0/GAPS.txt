Gate 0 observation audit: Rengar deck (decks/rengar.txt, sideboard excluded), 2026-11-19
====================================================================================
Scope: 1 legend, 1 champion, 17 main-deck cards, 3 battlefields, 2 runes, 2 tokens (Mech, Sand Soldier).
Input audited: rbenv observation = decision_export Encoder (smoke/data/src/decision_export.cpp:244-455):
g[72], card/bf/chain tokens (F=17), actions (K=46).
facts.json has 117 (card, fact) records: 54 OBSERVABLE, 39 DERIVABLE, 23 MISSING, 1 MISSING-but-unused.
Each record gives the source file:line, the field or derivation, and B2 coverage.
Generator: gen_facts.py. Prevalence counts: scan.py over smoke/data/out/base_teacher__s*
(151,827 recorded teacher decisions, Rengar and Yi decks).

MISSING facts, grouped by fix
-----------------------------
1. Token-unit vectors (B1). Cards: Ferrous Forerunner -> token:mech; Emperor's Dais -> token:sand_soldier.
   - Both keys are in vocab.json, but neither has a cards.json key or a B2 graph, so R is OOV.
   - Tags (Mech, Shurima) are not visible either.
   - A token unit is on the board in 33,547 decisions (22%).
   - An action names one in 6,760 decisions.
   - Sand Soldier alone appears in 72,618 teacher-train decisions (decision_stats.json).
2. Memory flags (A4). Cards: Star Spring, Pakaa Cub, Pyke (Hidden), Rampage, Darius.
   - Star Spring: the "first time each turn, per player" stamp (card_counters __starspring_*).
     A unit play to its slot is offered in 7,259 decisions, and the network cannot tell
     whether the trigger is still available.
   - hidden_on_turn: a facedown card is playable only on a later turn
     (game_object.h:75, game_engine.cpp:6127). Own facedown card present: 3,900 decisions.
   - Rampage paid flag (__rampage_paid) is lost after the cost prompt.
     Rampage is on the chain in 1,878 decisions; 229 of them are the opponent's to respond to.
   - Darius: the play ordinal of a spell already on the chain. Darius on board with a chain: 1,602 decisions.
   - Not needed: Pyke's paid flag is DERIVABLE, because its trigger reaches the chain only if paid.
3. Known opponent hand (A8). Card: Sabotage.
   - Only the NON-unit cards appear as tokens, and only during the choose prompt.
   - The revealed unit cards never appear.
   - Everything is forgotten afterwards.
   - 451 Sabotage prompts; 21,943 later decisions by a seat that had seen a reveal.
4. Deck knowledge (A9). Cards: Sabotage (the recycled card is known at the bottom of the opponent's deck);
   global (own remaining deck composition). Decision facts only.
5. Trash tokens (A1). Cards: Sabotage (inference about the opponent's remaining non-units); global.
   - Decision facts only: no in-scope card reads a trash.
6. Chain-item triggering subject (NEW). Cards: Pridestalker legend, Star Spring.
   - ChainItem.triggering_subject is not exported: which unit's play put the trigger on the chain.
   - Low impact.
7. Effective keywords / values (A3). Card: Inferna (temp_assault_value).
   - Rules-relevant only if the OPPONENT grants or removes keywords.
   - Every in-scope keyword is printed and static, so R(card) carries it.
   - Assault's effect is already in tok might once combat=1 (game_engine.cpp:7052-7059).
   - Own Inferna on board: 17,482 decisions.
8. Status flags (NEW, minor). Faefolk: cant_be_moved_by_enemy (game_object.h:378).
   Star Spring: moveToBaseBlocked. Both can be inferred only from the targets offered.
9. Battlefield statics (NEW, minor). Pakaa Cub, Pyke:
   - facedown_max_occupancy (game_state.h:464): not exported; always 1 in scope.
   - blocks_unit_play (Thrill's location filter): only via R(bf key).

DERIVABLE but costly (gate-1 candidates even though not strictly missing)
-------------------------------------------------------------------------
- Effective cost / affordable now (A6), for every hand card.
  - The net must compute: R cost - 2*[g69>=1] (Noxus Hopeful), + Accelerate [1][R] (Kai'Sa),
    + optional [O] (Rampage) / [R] (Pyke).
  - Then check that against the pool (g19-38) and the ready runes by domain (g39-62).
  - Noxus Hopeful is in own hand in 27,936 decisions.
- Move/play consequences (A7). Join act dst against the tok[b] slot, then sum Might per slot by controller.
  This feeds conquer (Kai'Sa, Dais, Seat), win combat (Nidalee), Faefolk's pull, Ganking (Pyke),
  Ambush placement and Thrill.
- Kinkou Initiate: the sum of own units' Might. Seat of Power: the count of controlled bfs. Both easy.

Priority (cards unblocked / decisions affected)
-----------------------------------------------
1. B1 token vectors: 2 cards + 2 tokens; ~22% of decisions have a token unit. Cheap: write 2 B2 graphs.
2. A4 memory flags, 5 cards:
   - Star Spring stamp: ~7.3k decisions.
   - hidden_on_turn: ~3.9k.
   - Rampage paid: ~1.9k.
   - Darius ordinal: ~1.6k.
3. A6 effective cost + affordable-now per hand card: affects every main-phase decision, every card.
   Derivable, but a 3-step computation; cheap to export.
4. A8 known opponent hand: 1 card; ~22k decisions after a reveal. Needs a knowledge tracker.
5. A7 move consequences: 8 cards. Derivable by a join, so lower priority than 1-4.
6. A9 / A1 deck and trash contents: decision-only, for all cards (inference). Larger input change.
7. A3 effective keywords: no in-deck source. Needed only against opponents that grant keywords.
8. Chain subject, status flags, bf statics: rare or constant in scope.

Surprises
---------
- B1 is not rare. Emperor's Dais makes Sand Soldiers often: token units are present in 22% of the decisions.
- Sabotage reveals the whole hand, but the encoder appends only the choosable non-unit cards
  (Encoder::ref via act ch). The revealed units are never visible, even during the prompt.
- Thrill's location prompt is answered by v = index into a FILTERED battlefield list
  (card_helpers.h:1926 valChoice), with no dst/bf field on the action.
  - Today the list is never filtered (0 of 472 prompts), so v == slot order.
  - A Rockfall-Path-like bf would silently shift the indices (C4-type action-reference hazard).
- Playing a unit from facedown and Thrill's replay both count as cards played
  (effect_executor.cpp:3224). So Darius / Legion / Pridestalker / Star Spring interact with Thrill.
  That is visible only via g69 and R.
- B2 errors and omissions versus the engine:
  - noxus_hopeful: ReduceCost target zone='board'; it applies in hand.
  - pit_rookie: no "only if unbuffed" condition.
  - pakaa_cub / pyke: Hidden play_mode lacks the "not the turn it was hidden" restriction.
  - irresistible_faefolk: no cant_be_moved_by_enemy filter.
  - thrill: dest any_battlefield is unfiltered, and the replay reset is not encoded.
  - grim_apothecary: no owner-vs-controller distinction.
  - emperor_s_dais: the token name is dropped (tag only).
  - Already flagged in the graphs: rampage (damage source), darius (token exclusion).
- B2 has graphs for Mech and Sand Soldier only as CreateToken payloads inside their creators.
  There is no standalone token graph, which is what B1 needs.

- The engine already computes much of what is missing per observer:
  - engine/observer_view.h (ObserverView) has effective keywords and assault (observer_view.cpp:109),
    lethal-by-cleanup-rule, spendable resources by purpose, known opponent hand cards,
    public piles in full, chain subjects, and the opponent's unseen pool (DeckModel).
  - The Encoder uses none of it.
  - Gate 1 could export ObserverView facts instead of re-deriving them.
